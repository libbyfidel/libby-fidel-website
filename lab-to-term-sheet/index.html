How venture capital actually works, what it needs from you, and how to tell whether it's the right money for what you're building.

Great technology isn't enough. You also need the right capital, raised at the right time, from people whose math fits yours.

I wrote this for researchers, engineers, and scientists turning hard technology into companies: chips, materials, energy, the physical stuff. Most fundraising advice is written for software. Deep tech runs on longer timelines and has different risks.

Each part has a tool in it. Drag, click, score yourself. Spend twenty minutes here and you'll walk into an investor meeting knowing what they're solving for.
