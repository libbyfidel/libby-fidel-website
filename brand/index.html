The pieces that make it look like me.

Logo marks, colors and type for anything carrying my name. Please use them as they are. Don't recolor, stretch, rotate or add effects. For other formats, email me.

01 — LOGO

The mark

An L with a Blaze full stop. Use the teal tile by default, the ink tile on photos or dark layouts, and the cream tile on teal.

Do

  • Leave clear space around the mark of at least half its width
  • Keep it 24px or larger on screen
  • Set the wordmark in Fraunces Regular

Don't

  • Recolor the tile or the Blaze dot
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows
  • Put it on busy photos without the ink tile
